## Question (exactly this, nothing more)
Do LINE-level scalar readings discriminate field-STATING lines from
register-matched verse hosts? — the necessary condition for any line-tier
claim in the paper (it validates the meter as a line-grain salience
instrument; it does NOT validate ghost calls — silent-but-high lines remain
unvalidated by this exam and stay exploratory regardless of outcome).

## Design
- **Positives (no new measurement):** every committed zh-side board line whose
  boolean FIRES for field F (the dictionary states it) — readings already
  committed in the _59 jsons. zh-only, to stay register-matched with the null
  (en positives vs zh hosts would repeat the register mismatch).
- **Negatives:** the 1,000 儒藏/诗经 tetrasyllabic hosts of the linecut_v2
  derivation (same declared harvest rule, re-encoded under the certificate
  law; anchor re-verified).
- **Adversarial caveat, declared:** hosts are real verse and contain genuine
  field content (关关雎鸠 states sound) — such hosts DEFLATE the measured
  AUC. The number reported is therefore conservative in that respect.
- **Fields:** color · plant · sound · illumination. Temporal EXCLUDED,
  declared: its zh receipts carry the 日/夕-radical over-reach found this
  night (labeler-audit shelf) — an exam on contaminated positives would be
  theater. (Her note of record: our temporal is temporal-duration, so
  calendrical receipts like 晦 are in-construct — the exclusion is caution,
  not a verdict.)
- **Statistic:** AUC of line readings, positives vs hosts, with bootstrap 95%
  CI (2,000 resamples, seed 48). n_pos reported per field; any field with
  n_pos < 15 reports "THIN — no exam" instead of a number.
- **Grades, pre-committed:** AUC lower-CI ≥ .75 = the line scalar earns a
  DISCRIMINATION statement at line grain (usable, tier-labeled, in the
  paper's methods); lower-CI in [.60, .75) = WEAK — line tier stays
  exploratory, stated as such; lower-CI < .60 = the line tier has no
  demonstrated discrimination — every line-tier surface in the paper retreats
  to design-description only. No post-hoc grade adjustment.
