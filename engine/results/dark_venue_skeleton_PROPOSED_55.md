# Venue-word illuminance skeleton (P3b, #55)

**STATUS: PROPOSED — PREP ONLY. Source list is LOCATE-ONLY: full citations, NO fetched values, NO invented lux.** Maps the dark pool's PLACE words (地窖/矿井/岩洞-class + the committed T3-scene members + her place-probes) to venue classes, each with a PROPOSED qualitative illuminance tendency and the citable standard that would license an actual lux figure. Acquiring any standard/number is the field owner's call.

**Circularity caveat (risk #1).** Every T3-scene member here was nominated via Places365 scene luminance and is therefore QUARANTINED for the exam (see `dark_nomination_split_PROPOSED_55.md`). These venue classes are for the PHYSICAL truth base only; a venue's illuminance must come from the ENGINEERING/DAYLIGHT standards below, never from the scene-luminance photo statistics that nominated it.

**Bucketing rule (deterministic).** Explicit override map first (boundary cases: 户外/室外/海洋公园→outdoor; 画室→studio; 水族馆/鱼缸→aquarium; 顶楼/阁楼→upper-floor; 牢房/禁闭→cell; 地下室/深海/洞穴/隧道/地窖→underground; 停电→power-cut), then ordered morpheme match (first class whose morpheme appears wins). Non-venue T3 members (verbs/abstract/shape — the F5 sense-noise class, risk #2) are listed out-of-scope.

## LOCATE-ONLY source list (citable illuminance standards — acquire on her call)

- **[S1]** EN 12464-1:2021 — *Light and lighting — Lighting of work places — Part 1: Indoor work places.* European Committee for Standardization (CEN), Brussels, 2021.
    - *Licenses:* Maintained illuminance (lux), UGR and uniformity for indoor task areas: offices, retail, restaurants/canteens, corridors/traffic zones, store rooms, industrial work areas, detention. Licenses lux for room_interior / retail_dining / corridor_passage / storage / industrial / cell classes.
- **[S2]** EN 12464-2:2014 — *Light and lighting — Lighting of work places — Part 2: Outdoor work places.* CEN, 2014.
    - *Licenses:* Illuminance for outdoor work areas and sports/industrial yards. Licenses outdoor_natural (worked) and outdoor industrial.
- **[S3]** CIE S 015/E:2005 — *Lighting of Outdoor Work Places.* International Commission on Illumination (CIE), Vienna, 2005.  (and CIE 150:2017, obtrusive light limits.)
    - *Licenses:* CIE-standard outdoor illuminance / obtrusive-light context for outdoor venues.
- **[S4]** CIE S 008/E:2001 = ISO 8995-1:2002 — *Lighting of indoor work places.* CIE/ISO.
    - *Licenses:* The CIE/ISO indoor illuminance schedule (task-dependent lux); cross-reference for indoor classes where EN 12464-1 is unavailable.
- **[S5]** IESNA/IES — *The Lighting Handbook: Reference and Application*, 10th ed. Illuminating Engineering Society of North America, New York, 2011.
    - *Licenses:* Recommended illuminance for a very broad venue list not fully covered by EN: theatres/auditoria (audience vs stage), museums/galleries (conservation limits), aquaria, studios, retail. Licenses performance_venue / gallery_museum / aquarium / studio.
- **[S6]** CIE S 011/E:2003 = ISO 15469:2004 — *Spatial distribution of daylight — CIE standard general sky.*  (with CIE 110:1994, reference sky luminance distributions.)
    - *Licenses:* Daylight availability / sky luminance → daylight illuminance indoors (daylight factor) and outdoors. Licenses outdoor_natural and upper_floor (daylight-fraction).
- **[S7]** Natural-illuminance reference tables — e.g. Schlyter, P., *Radiometry and photometry in astronomy* (illuminance of full daylight, overcast, the three twilights, full moon, starlight); and the IES *Lighting Handbook* reference-volume natural-illuminance table.
    - *Licenses:* The lux ranges for daylight / twilight / night SKY conditions. Ties artifact (a)'s time-word classes (noon/daylight/twilight/deep-night) to actual illuminance. LOCATE; do not transcribe numbers here.
- **[S8]** Underground-mine illumination regulation — e.g. US 30 CFR §75.1719 (illumination of working places, underground coal mines, MSHA); ILO/relevant national mine-lighting codes.
    - *Licenses:* Floor illuminance in worked underground spaces. Licenses underground (mines/shafts) where a worked-space number is wanted.
- **[S9]** Astronomical twilight definitions — USNO / NOAA Solar Calculator: civil / nautical / astronomical twilight at solar elevation −6° / −12° / −18°; solar noon = max elevation.
    - *Licenses:* Fixes the SOLAR-ELEVATION boundaries behind artifact (a)'s twilight vs deep-night vs daylight vs noon classes (bilingual, exact, non-photographic).
