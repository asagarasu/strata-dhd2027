# CONVERGENCE PROBE: brooding/troubled-pondering in ordinary modern usage.
# STRICTLY BANNED: color, dark/light, water/surge vocabulary — the test is whether
# the concept drags the sensory strands along WITHOUT the words being present.
# (Fossil metaphors of other strands — mull=grind, brüten=incubate — are allowed:
# they are how the concept is actually lexicalized.)
Er grübelte die halbe Nacht über der Nachricht.
Sie brütete stundenlang über dem Brief.
Er wälzte die Frage wieder und wieder im Kopf.
Sie zermarterte sich den Kopf über seine Worte.
Er hing schweren Gedanken nach.
Sie saß schweigend da und grübelte vor sich hin.
Die Frage ging ihm unablässig im Kopf herum.
Er brütete über dem, was sie gesagt hatte.
He brooded over the letter half the night.
She turned his words over and over in her mind.
He sat in silence, brooding on the news.
The question would not leave her mind all day.
She kept mulling over what he had said.
He dwelt on the remark for days.
She was lost in troubled thought.
He could not stop ruminating on the decision.
