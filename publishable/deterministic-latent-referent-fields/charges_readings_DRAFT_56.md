## Sonnet 73 (the pilot; committed #55 numbers)
All four renderings add written colour at positions where the source is
colour-silent. In three of them (梁宗岱, 屠岸, 梁实秋) one addition is
live according to the detector we built — 黑夜 (+5.55, call True) — and
one is not: 青春 (−0.93) is written-claimed but text-silent in all four.
The fourth rendering (辜正坤) lacks 黑夜 and reaches its one live charge
through a different word entirely: 火种 (+2.83).
