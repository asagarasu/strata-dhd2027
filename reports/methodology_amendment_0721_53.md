## 1. The field architecture, then the latent construct
*(Frame restored 2026-07-21, her catch: this amendment details the
LATENT rows because that is what changed — it does not replace the
field architecture.)* Every field's deterministic layer is the
umbrella: **deterministic-F = descriptive-F + latent-F**. The
DESCRIPTIVE row — the field openly present in the wording — keeps
its standing sensors and credentials unchanged by this amendment:
the word-level field booleans (citable lexicon derivations) and the
battery-credentialed scalar rulers (color .879, schematic .825,
symbolic-register, sound .815, plant .801, temporal ρ.860) score it
as they always have. The reflective layer is PROVISIONALLY out of
scope — out for good only if no measurement is found within days
(her clause, 07-21: the empirical exit stays open). What follows concerns the latent row only.

A word can carry a field (color, illumination, sound, plant,
temporal) without its dictionary meaning saying so. This latency
has two distinct carriers, measured as separate rows (ruling 07-20):

- **latent-written** — the field rides the visible written form:
  the character 赤 (red) inside 赤字 "fiscal deficit"; the etymon
  sidus (star) inside English "consider"; κάλχη (the murex) inside
  καλχαίνω "to brood." 釋字-level meaning.
- **latent-referent** — the field rides world-knowledge of the
  referent: tomatoes are red, though no clause of 西红柿's
  dictionary meaning states it.

The rows have opposite translation-survival defaults, which is the
rubric's gain: referent meaning survives translation automatically
(the tomato stays red in any language); written meaning dies at a
script boundary unless the translator COMPENSATES. A written-row
survival score is therefore a compensation-event detector; a
referent-row score is a default-survival baseline. The two rows can
take different survival verdicts on one word in one translation —
exactly the discrimination the interface-conformance rubric scores.

  battery-credentialed descriptive axis, read at the line), the

## 3. The in-situ instrument (one mechanism, one example)
Isolated readings of characters are structurally invalid (the
tokenizer boundary: no containing character inherits its
component's axis charge; 07-20 component batch). Latent charge is
therefore measured in host sentences by matched substitution:

    delta(s, w′) = axis(embed(s)) − axis(embed(s[w→w′]))
    charge(word) = median over hosts s of [ median over ensemble w′ of delta(s, w′) ]

Example: for 波黑 (Bosnia; 黑 = black, print-silent at word level),
hosts are corpus sentences containing 波黑; substitutes are
same-head-sememe words with the charged character swapped
(波斯-class), generated by a committed citable rule; the color-axis
delta that survives the double median is 波黑's in-context latent
color charge. Null = the identical procedure over uncharged control
words. Call threshold z ≥ 1.5 vs the null; floors F1 ≥ .70;
registered before running; aborts published.

*(2026-07-21, construct pivot; session53_note "Sittings 6-7";
impression_norms/PROVENANCE.)* A single character that surfaces only
inside compounds has no standalone host to substitute in (雪 carries
no standalone token in the norms — present only as 雪花 / 雪山; 夜 is
met as 深夜 / 夜景, not bare 夜). Such a character is measured as a
GROUP NODE (夜-group): membership = the compounds whose word-level
sememe tree OVERLAPS the character's own (深夜 qualifies; 夜叉 does
not — HowNet-mechanical, no judgement made); the field charge
attributes to the group, per-compound detail beneath it, and the
transmission-ratio becomes within-group structure rather than a
single-token survival number.

## 4. The composition algorithm (scoring a unit)
1. Word-level field boolean fires (word's own entry carries the
   field) → DESCRIPTIVE. Stop.
2. Boolean silent ∧ scalar charge high → latent candidate:
   a. written sensor fires → LATENT-WRITTEN (carrier cited);
   b. else, a CITABLE referent account exists for the word (its
      own-language definition names the field, or the pixel witness
      at X) → LATENT-REFERENT — *(norm entries REMOVED from the
      trigger list 2026-07-21 evening, her word: truth sources are
      truth-ONLY — a norm set may credential a word or trigger it,
      never both for the same word; the pre-CCFD text predated a norm
      shelf rich enough to hold the truth column alone)*
      — *(re-restated 2026-07-21 after her circularity objection)*
      the TRIGGER is the citable evidence, per word, exactly as the
      written row's trigger is its citable carrier; the credentialed
      meter then supplies the DEGREE (charge, survival scoring).
      Three roles, three sources, no circle: citable evidence
      triggers the row; the meter measures it; the norms credential
      the meter. A charge with no citable account cannot classify
      referent on the meter's word alone;
   c. no citable account at all (no carrier, no witness, no norm —
      the meter alone speaks) → UNATTRIBUTED, to the ledger with
      the two-flavor annotation: witness-gap (re-attributable in
      principle — the 银杏 class) vs reflective-candidate
      (structurally unwitnessable — the conjectured reflective
      shadow).
   Degree-reporting on the referent row is INERT until attempt-4
   credentials the meter; the citable triggers stand regardless.
The unattributed remainder is conjectured (her formulation, 07-21)
to be the reflective flavor held provisionally out of scope: in the
adopted Kantian frame (the collaborator 07-20, adapted from KU §49/§59),
the sensors read word-borne symbol-tracks — convention-warranted,
measurable; ignition of an aesthetic idea is the reader's own event,
provisionally unmeasured — the exclusion is EMPIRICAL, not
principled: it stands only if no instrument is found within days
(her clause, 07-21). The instrument measures the fuel; the
burning is the reader's. Translation carries wording, schemata, and
symbol-tracks; trait survival measures exactly the portion of the
poem that travels between languages.

## 5. What was tried and does not work (brief, with records)

  characters; killed structurally, replaced by §3.

  pools_definition_leg_v4_53; reader_truth_reread_53).* Three

## 6. Ancestry (what is taken; what is claimed)
The two-row distinction is ancestral and assembled here, not
invented: Ullmann's morphological motivation and the 理據 tradition
(王宁 2015; 六書) anticipate latent-written; Haiman (1980) and
Langacker's encyclopedic semantics legitimize latent-referent;
Potebnja's inner form names the fading this project measures at
script boundaries; Berman (1985) names "the destruction of the
letter" qualitatively; Hervey & Higgins (1992) and Newmark supply
the compensation taxonomy the survival score detects against;
semantic-radical priming shows the written row is cognitively real.
(Full map: reports/lit_ancestry_two_row_53.md.) Claimed as new:
the two rows as separately operable measurements; per-language
citable sensors; survival scored as a rate; the structural
survival asymmetry stated; compensation events detected
algorithmically.
