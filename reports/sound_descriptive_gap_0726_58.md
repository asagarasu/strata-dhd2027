# GAP: the direct-description sound labeler was never built (2026-07-26)

Surfaced at her review of the L5 exhibit. Recorded before anything else is
claimed about the sound field.

## The gap, plainly
The delivered "descriptive sound" boolean is DEVICE-tier only (CMU
allit/word-rep en · 叠字/雙聲/聯綿 zh; corpus-wide receipt kinds: allit,
rep, 叠字). A labeler answering "is this word a direct description of
sound" (噪/歌声/noise/singing) does not exist anywhere in the pipeline.
Every summary that said "descriptive row fully credentialed ×5 fields" —
including #58's methodology sheet yesterday — rounded this substitution
into a tick. The ledger recorded "sound-device" honestly in one line of
§6; every downstream table dropped the qualifier.

## Why it matters for the paper (not cosmetic)
1. The draft's H2 says a schema element "may be STATED in the wording."
   For four fields stated = mentioned. For sound, stated = ENACTED
   (device). Same word, two constructs — unflagged.
2. The revival finding's reading depends on it. Source L5 弦歌聲 MENTIONS
   sound; a word-tier labeler would plausibly fire there → source becomes
   stated → the five xibei "revivals" re-read as survivals of stated
   sound. Whether the headline survives depends on which construct the
   paper claims.
3. The written-tier carriers for sound (商·曲·彈·歌·音; the 171→200 char
   list includes cry|喊-class) are sound-MEANING graphs, not euphony
   graphs — so the current "revival" is a crossing BETWEEN tiers
   (meaning-carried → device-enacted). Whether that is one field or two
   is a construct ruling nobody has made.

## Two lanes (her call, with the collaborator)
A. BUILD the word-tier labeler (zh: HowNet DEF sound-head words; en:
   WordNet auditory closure — the referent-trigger inventories are
   partial precursors), re-run the descriptive pass, re-verdict. Data
   work of unknown-but-boundable size; touches committed verdicts;
   post-freeze lane rules apply. Pre-08-01 feasibility unknown.
B. PRECISION-REFRAME the draft: everywhere sound is discussed, "stated"
   → "enacted as device"; the finding becomes device-revival; the
   missing mention-tier is declared as a coverage gap ("sound-mention
   descriptive channel: unbuilt; mentions currently route to referent
   tier"). Honest, cheap, survives review only if the tier-mixing in
   point 3 is also stated.

Neither lane is chosen here. The four-readers H1 issue and this one are
the same failure shape at project scale: completion language rounding a
construct gap into a checkmark. Two instances are a pattern; summaries
must carry qualifiers forward, not polish them off.
